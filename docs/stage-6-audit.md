# Stage 6 — pain-point resolver audit

Date: 2026-08-26

The native Eyeson worker now resolves blocked/high-friction evidence into structured
`UXPainPoint` records. It preserves observed facts separately from behavioral effects
and inferred diagnosis, calculates versioned element-friction overlays, attributes the
acted and changed stable elements, and aggregates repeated signatures into episodes.

Fixture acceptance verifies that a validation error highlights the exact acted button
box and retains the screenshot, step, timestamp, state deltas, and classifier evidence.
The coefficients remain simulation metrics rather than claims of human calibration.
